Train your brain with fun mini games that challenge your memory, focus, logic and math skills. Braincup offers 45 brain training exercises to sharpen your mind and improve concentration.

<b>Mental Calculation</b>:
Follow the math expressions. Use each result as the base for the next.

<b>Chain Calculation</b>:
Solve complex formulas step by step.

<b>Sherlock Calculation</b>:
Find a formula to reach the goal using only the given numbers and operators.

<b>Fraction Calculation</b>:
Solve the fractions.

<b>Value Comparison</b>:
Select the expression with the highest value.

<b>Bubble Sum</b>:
Add the numbers on the floating bubbles.

<b>Quick Sum</b>:
Numbers flash one at a time. Add them up and enter the total.

<b>Anomaly Puzzle</b>:
Find the figure that stands out.

<b>Pattern Sequence</b>:
Work out the rule running along each row, then pick the piece that completes it.

<b>Path Finder</b>:
Start at the marked cell and follow the arrows to the destination.

<b>Visual Memory</b>:
Memorize the shapes, then identify the hidden one when the timer ends.

<b>Ghost Grid</b>:
Watch the tiles light up one by one, then tap them back in the same order.

<b>Color Confusion</b>:
Tap the color a word is printed in, not the color the word names.

<b>Colored Shapes</b>:
Add up the points from the correct statements.

<b>Orbit Tracker</b>:
Watch the highlighted balls, then track them as they move.

<b>Flash Crowd</b>:
Dots flash briefly on each side. Tap the side that had more dots.

<b>Mini Sudoku</b>:
Fill the grid so every row, column and block holds each number exactly once.

<b>Mini Chess</b>:
Checkmate the CPU before it checkmates you.

<b>Solo Chess</b>:
Capture pieces until only the king remains. Every move must capture.

<b>Lights Out</b>:
Turn off every light. Each tap toggles the cell and its four neighbours.

<b>Sliding Puzzle</b>:
Slide tiles into the empty space to put them back in order.

<b>Shikaku</b>:
Divide the grid into rectangles, each holding one number equal to its area.

<b>Nurikabe</b>:
Paint the sea around numbered islands, never forming a 2x2 block.

<b>Cat Queens</b>:
Place one cat per row, column and color zone. No two may touch, not even diagonally.

<b>Knot</b>:
Connect each pair of colored dots. Paths may not cross and must fill every cell.

<b>Tower of Hanoi</b>:
Move the full stack to the right peg.

<b>Schulte Table</b>:
Tap the numbers in order from 1 upwards as fast as you can.

<b>Flags</b>:
Identify the country by its flag.

<b>Digit Memory</b>:
Memorize a digit sequence, solve a quick math problem, then recall it.

<b>Spot the New</b>:
Tap the animal you have not seen before.

<b>N-Back</b>:
Tap Match when the shape repeats the one N steps back.

<b>Wordle</b>:
Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

<b>Matchstick Riddles</b>:
Move matchsticks to fix broken equations.

<b>Peg Solitaire</b>:
Jump pegs to clear the board down to one peg.

<b>Sudoku</b>:
Classic full-size Sudoku with multiple difficulties.

<b>Chess</b>:
Full chess vs CPU or pass-and-play with a friend.

<b>IQ Test</b>:
30 matrix puzzles in 15 minutes, with an estimated IQ. Just for fun.

<b>Missing Operators</b>:
Find the missing operators (+, -, *, /) that make the equation true.

<b>Bulls & Cows</b>:
Guess the secret 4-digit number with unique digits.

<b>Simon Says</b>:
Each round adds one pad. Tap the whole sequence back from the start.

<b>Prism Clear</b>:
Swap tiles to clear lines of three or more. Empty the board to win.

<b>Trio</b>:
Find three cards that share a shape, a count or a fill.

<b>Mental Rotations</b>:
Is the second block the first one turned, or mirrored?

<b>Mental Flex</b>:
The pair at the top says which trait counts: shape or color.

<b>Rule Shift</b>:
Sort each card onto a key card. Nobody tells you the rule, and it changes once you have it.

Several <b>achievements</b>, and every exercise has <b>medals</b> to keep you motivated.

No tracking. <b>Open source</b>.
