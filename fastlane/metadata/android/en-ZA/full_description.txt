Mini-games for memory, focus, logic and math, plus Learn Math lessons with certificates.

<b>Mental Calculation</b>:
Follow expressions; each result is the base for the next.

<b>Chain Calculation</b>:
Solve a formula step by step.

<b>Sherlock Calculation</b>:
Reach the goal with the given numbers and operators.

<b>Fraction Calculation</b>:
Solve the fractions.

<b>Value Comparison</b>:
Pick the expression with the highest value.

<b>Bubble Sum</b>:
Add the numbers on the floating bubbles.

<b>Quick Sum</b>:
Numbers flash one at a time; enter the total.

<b>Anomaly Puzzle</b>:
Find the figure that stands out.

<b>Pattern Sequence</b>:
Work out the rule along each row and complete the grid.

<b>Path Finder</b>:
Follow the arrows from the marked start.

<b>Visual Memory</b>:
Memorize the shapes, then identify the hidden one.

<b>Ghost Grid</b>:
Watch tiles light up; tap them back in order.

<b>Color Confusion</b>:
Tap the ink color, not the color the word names.

<b>Colored Shapes</b>:
Add the points from the correct statements.

<b>Orbit Tracker</b>:
Track the highlighted balls as they move.

<b>Flash Crowd</b>:
Tap the side that flashed more dots.

<b>Mini Sudoku</b>:
Each row, column and block once per digit.

<b>Mini Chess</b>:
Finish the scenario as white against the CPU.

<b>Solo Chess</b>:
Capture until only the king remains. Every move must capture.

<b>Lights Out</b>:
Turn off every light; taps toggle neighbours.

<b>Sliding Puzzle</b>:
Slide tiles into order.

<b>Shikaku</b>:
Divide the grid into rectangles matching their numbers.

<b>Nurikabe</b>:
Paint the sea around numbered islands, no 2x2 blocks.

<b>Cat Queens</b>:
One cat per row, column and color zone; none may touch.

<b>Knot</b>:
Connect colored pairs without crossing; cover every cell.

<b>Tower of Hanoi</b>:
Move the stack to the right peg.

<b>Schulte Table</b>:
Tap the numbers in order as fast as you can.

<b>Flags</b>:
Name the country; one wrong answer ends the run.

<b>Digit Memory</b>:
Memorize digits, solve a quick sum, then recall.

<b>Spot the New</b>:
Tap the animal you have not seen before.

<b>N-Back</b>:
Tap Match when a shape repeats the one N steps back.

<b>Wordle</b>:
Guess the five-letter word in six tries.

<b>Matchstick Riddles</b>:
Move matchsticks to fix equations.

<b>Peg Solitaire</b>:
Jump pegs until one remains.

<b>Sudoku</b>:
Classic full-size Sudoku, several difficulties.

<b>Chess</b>:
Full chess vs the CPU or pass-and-play.

<b>IQ Test</b>:
30 matrix puzzles in 15 minutes, with an estimated IQ. Just for fun.

<b>Missing Operators</b>:
Find the operators (+, -, *, /) that make the equation true.

<b>Bulls & Cows</b>:
Guess the secret 4-digit number.

<b>Simon Says</b>:
Tap the growing pad sequence back each round.

<b>Prism Clear</b>:
Swap matching tiles to clear lines; empty the board.

<b>Trio</b>:
Find three cards that share a shape, a fill or a count.

<b>Mental Rotations</b>:
Is the second block the first one turned, or mirrored?

<b>Mental Flex</b>:
A cue pair sets the rule; match the target on that trait.

<b>Rule Shift</b>:
Sort cards by a hidden rule that changes once you have it.

<b>Reversi</b>:
6x6 vs the CPU or pass-and-play. Trap a line to flip it.

<b>Learn Math</b>:
Lessons, tests and certificates in Arithmetic, Geometry, Measurement and Algebra.

Every game has <b>medals</b>. Several <b>achievements</b>. No tracking. <b>Open source</b>.
